The longer version

Two careers. Two children. One messy, full life.

I spend my weekdays in two places that look nothing alike: a quiet adjusting room where I work with patients one at a time, and a lecture hall where I teach Gross Anatomy to a hundred first-years who are mostly terrified of the lab.

I spend my evenings and most of my weekends with my husband and our two kids, doing the very ordinary things — laundry, library runs, lentil soup, bedtime stories that get read four times. This blog is the space in between.
